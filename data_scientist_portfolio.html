Garlic Farming Intelligence

Agriculture analytics
From farm data
to better decisions.

Project goal

Explore how data analysis could help garlic growers understand production costs, market prices, potential revenue, and profitability before making farming decisions.

  • Record farm inputs and production costs.
  • Compare market prices when reliable, dated data is available.
  • Build a simple profit and return-on-investment calculator.
  • Visualize trends and clearly explain the limits of any estimates.
Current status: concept/prototype. The chart is illustrative, not real garlic-market data. Forecasts will require verified historical data and field validation.
